## Design
A TV API30-only backdrop producer owns an isolated recording of visible base content. Exclude the rail and focused-material overlays from that recording; keep normal visible content rendering intact. Downsample a bounded image, blur on Default dispatcher using reusable existing pixel blur, publish an immutable generation-tagged result with source/root coordinates. Record/readback only on content invalidation and bounded throttle, coalesce updates, one job in flight, invalidate immediately on navigation, discard stale route/size results. No polling capture every display frame and no screenshot of whole composed window containing overlays. Reader material uses current sampled coordinates and thin neutral rim/sheens; no fixed black strip/dense0.62 scrim or high-opacity fallback masking actual backdrop. API31+ accelerated/mobile behavior retained. Full-screen video/surface view recording limitations explicitly scoped; required acceptance Home/Search/Settings.
